# Content inventory and provenance

## Required before public clinic launch
- Legal clinic name, location/address, phone, email, opening hours.
- Licensed provider names, biographies, credentials, professional portraits (with permission).
- Actual treatments/services, durations, prices (if publicly displayed).
- Real clinic photographs, brand logo, approved marketing copy.
- Valid privacy notice, booking and cancellation policies.

## Demo / portfolio-mode content
- Fictional clinic name Dentora, illustrated or appropriately licensed non-patient imagery.
- Fictional dentists labelled as demo professionals.
- Sample services/durations flagged as demonstration values.
- No fabricated testimonials, review ratings, awards, professional memberships or clinical success claims.
- Clear banner/footnote that booking data is demo data and should not be used for medical care.

## Content ownership
- Marketing text: clinic owner approval before real release.
- Image assets: copyright/license documented.
- Clinical assertions: qualified professional review before publication.
